# Frozen high-spread collision convention

The production high-spread collision calculation was frozen with:

- 65-qubit distance-5 heavy-hex graph
- 72 coupling edges
- nominal frequencies: 5.00, 5.07, 5.14 GHz
- nominal spacing: 70 MHz
- transmon anharmonicity: -330 MHz
- 35 degree-two controls assigned to the high frequency group
- same nominal frequency plan for spatial and shuffled conditions
- no per-condition optimization
- later IBM/LASIQ symmetric Type-4 convention

The Type-4 convention was later audited against two alternative interpretations. The primary spatial effect retained the same sign under all three tested modes.
